Tunisia has the talent. What we need is more people who actually ship.

<!-- more -->

Every time I talk to another Tunisian developer or student, I hear the same thing: good ideas, great potential, but projects that never see the light of day. We have the skills — our engineers work at top companies globally. But at home, the startup ecosystem is still finding its footing.

## The gap

There's a gap between _having an idea_ and _shipping a product_. It's the gap between theory and practice. And it's the gap I want to help close.

Shipping means:
- Putting a product in front of real users
- Getting feedback (even when it stings)
- Iterating until it works
- Being okay with "embarrassingly minimal" v1

## Why it matters

When more Tunisians ship products:
- We build a portfolio of local solutions to local problems
- We create a culture of building, not just consuming
- We show the next generation that it's possible
- We contribute to the global tech conversation from Tunisia

## My commitment

Every product I ship will be:
1. Built in the open
2. Solving a real problem (ideally local)
3. A learning experience I document and share

This site is v1. More coming.

If you're a Tunisian builder, student, or dreamer — let's connect. The ecosystem grows when we build together.
