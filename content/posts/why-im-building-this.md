A personal site is more than a portfolio — it's a place to think out loud, document the journey, and invite others along.

<!-- more -->

I've been a student long enough to know that the real learning happens when you build. When you ship. When you put your ideas out there and let the world push back.

This site is that space for me.

## Why a personal site?

1. **Write to think** — The best way to clarify an idea is to write it down. This is my public notebook.
2. **Document the journey** — From student to builder to researcher. I want to look back and see the progress.
3. **Connect with people** — Tunisia has a growing tech scene. I want to find the people who are building, thinking, and shipping.

## What you'll find here

- **Tech & building** — Products I'm shipping, tools I'm using, lessons learned
- **Research** — My journey learning to read and publish papers
- **Philosophy** — Ideas that shape how I think about tech and life
- **Life** — Basketball, anime, movies, music — the stuff that keeps me going

## Ship or it didn't happen

There's a quote I keep coming back to:

> _"The minimum viable product is the first thing you're embarrassed to show people."_

This site is that. It's minimal, it's a little rough, but it's mine. And it'll get better as I do.

Let's build.
